Yes—the AI niche is viable, but “AI” itself is too broad to be a defensible niche.

The viable angle is a specific audience with a specific outcome, for example:

- AI for small businesses: save time on sales, support, content, and admin.
- AI for creators: scripts, editing, research, repurposing.
- AI for job seekers: portfolios, interview prep, automation.
- AI for a vertical: real estate, renovation, education, legal ops, e-commerce.

What is crowded: generic AI news, tool roundups, and “10 ChatGPT prompts” content.  
What still works: practical workflows, before/after results, templates, implementation help, and a recognizable point of view.

A fast viability test: if you can name (1) a buyer/audience, (2) an expensive or recurring problem, and (3) a measurable result AI helps deliver, it is viable. If your pitch is only “I talk about AI,” it will be hard to differentiate.

Best five-minute decision: proceed, but narrow to “AI for [specific person] to achieve [specific result].”
