Yes—the AI niche is viable, but “AI” itself is far too broad to win in now.

The strongest opportunities are specific, outcome-driven angles: AI for a single profession, workflow, or painful business task. Examples: AI for local-service marketing, sales follow-up, real-estate content, legal/admin paperwork, e-commerce listings, customer support, or short-form video production.

Avoid generic “AI news,” basic prompt tips, and broad tool roundups unless you already have distribution. They are crowded and easily copied.

A fast viability test:

- Clear buyer: one identifiable audience with money or urgency.
- Repeated pain: a task they do weekly, not a novelty.
- Measurable result: saves time, increases leads, reduces cost, or improves output.
- Narrow entry point: solve one workflow exceptionally well.
- Distribution edge: existing audience, industry access, SEO niche, partnerships, or strong content format.

My blunt take: viable if you position it as “AI that helps [specific person] achieve [specific result],” not “an AI product/channel.” The market is still growing, but generic AI offerings are becoming commodities fast.
